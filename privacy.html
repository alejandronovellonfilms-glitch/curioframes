Privacy Policy – CurioFrames Studio

Last updated: 30 October 2026

CurioFrames Studio runs only on the owner's own computer. The owner is its only user.

Google data the app uses

  • YouTube Data API: to upload the owner's videos to the owner's channel, to read their status, and to change their scheduled publish date.
  • Google Drive API: to store the owner's own video files so the owner's scheduling services can fetch them.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens and video data stay on the owner's computer. The app does not send Google user data to third parties, does not sell it, and does not use it for advertising. It does not collect data about other people.

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove the app's access at any time at Google account permissions.
